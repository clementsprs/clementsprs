# Hi, I'm Clément

GTM builder. I lead marketing at [Claap](https://www.claap.io) (by [lemlist](https://www.lemlist.com)), and most of what I ship is software: the pages, campaigns, games and AI agents that bring sales teams in.

## What I do

- **Positioning and product marketing:** product pages, competitor comparison pages, launch messaging, EN and FR.
- **Acquisition:** paid social and search (LinkedIn, Meta, Google Ads), SEO and GEO (getting cited by ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity).
- **Lifecycle and outbound:** product newsletters and launch emails, cold sequences in lemlist, signal-based prospecting.
- **Conversion:** landing pages and A/B tests on the homepage, contact and product heroes.
- **Creative:** product motion films and ad videos (Remotion, Runway), static ads.
- **Data:** funnel and pipeline reporting across HubSpot, GA4, Amplitude and Metabase.
- **Building:** I use Claude Code to turn GTM ideas into shipped products, agents and automations.
